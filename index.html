Why two cuisines, one box

The old spice roads never really closed.

Long before "fusion" was a menu word, the caravans that carried cardamom out of the Levant were the same ones that carried it toward the Yellow Sea. Sumac and gochujang, olive oil and sesame — they've been trading shelf space for a thousand years. Secret Recipe is the box where that overlap lives: dishes our families actually cooked, written the way a grandmother dictates them — by feel, then corrected until they're exact.

No lifestyle photography, no fifteen-part storytelling. Every card lists what it takes, what it costs in time, and where it came from. If a recipe doesn't earn its place in the drawer, it doesn't go in.

Why we don't hold seats for walk-ins

Ten stools around one counter means there is no "back of house" capacity to lean on. One no-show reservation is one-tenth of the room. So we book tight, and we book ahead — that's what keeps the kitchen able to cook everything to order instead of holding food for tables that may or may not fill.

  • 01Dinner only. One seating a night, 5:30–9:30pm. No lunch, no brunch.
  • 02Book at least 48 hours out where you can — weekend seats go first.
  • 03Parties of 6 or fewer. Larger groups won't fit the counter — email us instead.
  • 04Running late? We can hold a seat 15 minutes past your time, not longer.
About walk-ins: we'll seat you if a stool happens to be open when you arrive — but with ten seats a night, that's rare, and reserved guests always come first. A reservation is the only reliable way in.
